# Fantasy Premier League (FPL) Rules

## 1. Squad Selection
* **Squad Size**: 15 players (2 Goalkeepers, 5 Defenders, 5 Midfielders, 3 Forwards).
* **Budget**: The total value of your initial squad must not exceed £100.0 million.
* **Players Per Team**: You can select up to 3 players from a single Premier League team.

## 2. Managing Your Team
* **Starting XI**: Choose 11 starting players each Gameweek. The formation must include at least 1 Goalkeeper, 3 Defenders, and 1 Forward.
* **Substitutes**: Order your 4 bench players. If a starter plays 0 minutes, the highest-priority sub who played will automatically replace them (respecting formation limits).
* **Captain & Vice-Captain**: Your captain's points are doubled. If your captain plays 0 minutes, the vice-captain's points are doubled instead. If both play 0 minutes, no player gets doubled points.

## 3. Transfers
* **Free Transfers**: You receive 1 free transfer each Gameweek.
* **Rolling Transfers**: You can roll unused free transfers. FPL allows banking up to 5 free transfers.
* **Point Deductions (Hits)**: Any additional transfers beyond your free allocation cost -4 points per transfer, deducted from your Gameweek score.
* **Player Prices**: Player prices fluctuate based on transfer market activity. A 50% sell-on fee applies to profits made on players.

## 4. Chips
You can play only one chip per Gameweek:
* **Wildcard (x2)**: Make unlimited free transfers. One available in the first half of the season, one in the second.
* **Free Hit**: Make unlimited free transfers for one Gameweek only. Your team reverts to its previous state for the next Gameweek.
* **Bench Boost**: The points scored by your 4 bench players are included in your Gameweek total.
* **Triple Captain**: Your captain's points are tripled instead of doubled.
* **Mystery Chip**: A new chip (details usually revealed later in the season).

## 5. Scoring Points
Players score points for:
* **Playing**: 1 pt (up to 60 mins), 2 pts (60+ mins).
* **Goals Scored**: Goalkeeper/Defender (6 pts), Midfielder (5 pts), Forward (4 pts).
* **Assists**: 3 pts for all positions.
* **Clean Sheets**: Goalkeeper/Defender (4 pts), Midfielder (1 pt) - must play at least 60 minutes.
* **Saves**: 1 pt per 3 saves (Goalkeepers).
* **Penalty Saves**: 5 pts (Goalkeepers).
* **Bonus Points System (BPS)**: The 3 best performing players in a match receive 3, 2, and 1 bonus points.
* **Deductions**: Yellow card (-1), Red card (-3), Own goal (-2), Penalty miss (-2), For every 2 goals conceded by a GK/DEF (-1).
